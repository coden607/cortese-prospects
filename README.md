# Cortese Prospects — Sales Pipeline

**Owner:** Stephen Blanford — Binghamton, NY
**Product:** Missed-call → text-back / order-recovery service for restaurants.

## Thesis
Every busy restaurant loses real order revenue every single day to busy signals, hold-time hangups, and after-hours missed calls. This service recovers those orders: busy-line forwarding to our system, automatic text-back within seconds, counter tablet so staff see and reclaim callers, and a clean recovered-revenue report. **$0 upfront. 15% of recovered sales only.** The restaurant only pays out of money we gave back to them — the pitch sells itself at 2–4pm when the lunch rush just proved the pain.

## Sourcing Method
- Leads are independent and mini-chain restaurants in the Southern Tier of NY + Northern Tier of PA (Binghamton, Johnson City, Endicott, Endwell, Vestal, Owego, Cortland, Ithaca, Syracuse, Utica-Rome, Elmira-Corning; Sayre/Athens PA; Scranton/Wilkes-Barre PA).
- Categories: pizza & takeout, Italian, Chinese/Japanese takeout, wings/BBQ, busy diners, and 3–5 regional mini-chains.
- Every row carries a `source_url` pointing at the business's own website/Facebook page or a reputable public listing (e.g., official directory). **No invented phone numbers — ever.** Unverifiable rows are dropped, not guessed.

## Legal / Compliance Note
- All business contact info used is **publicly available business information** (storefront listings, official sites, public Facebook pages). No consumer data is collected.
- Outreach happens during **2–4pm local business hours** (post-lunch-rush window).
- Every email carries a CAN-SPAM footer: *"— Stephen Blanford · Binghamton, NY · reply STOP to opt out"* and every opt-out is honored immediately and permanently.
