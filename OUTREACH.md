# OUTREACH KIT — Busy-Line Recovery Engine

*The offer: when your phone lines are busy, we text the caller back instantly and turn missed rings into confirmed orders. $0 upfront, no monthly fee, no contract — 15% of recovered sales only. Nothing changes on your website, register, or phone bills.*

## The one-paragraph offer (use everywhere)
> "When both your lines are busy, your callers get a busy signal — and about 8 out of 10 hang up instead of leaving a message. I install busy-forwarding with your phone company (one setting, reversible any time), and your busy callers instantly get a text-back. Your staff confirm the order on a counter tablet. You pay nothing up front and nothing monthly — I take 15% of orders that come in through this and get picked up. Worst case is exactly what happens today."

## The door-opener: the Busy-Signal Demo
1. **Friday 5:45–7:15pm**, call the prospect's takeout line from your mobile.
2. If you get through, try once more in 15 minutes. If it's busy even once → screenshot the call log showing "Busy" at peak hour.
3. Open every pitch with it: *"I tried to give you money Friday at 6:12 and got a busy signal. I'm not mad — I'm a guy from Binghamton who fixes exactly that, and you keep 85 cents of every dollar I recover."*
4. If it's NEVER busy, that's data too — deprioritize them. The demo qualifies the lead in one call.

## Cold-call script (owners, 2–4pm only — never rush hours)
> "Hi, is the owner or manager around? — Hey, this is Stephen, I'm local, Binghamton. Quick reason for the call: I build a thing that catches the phone orders your place misses when both lines ring at dinner. It costs you nothing to try — I only get paid a small cut of orders I actually recover, and you can turn it off with one phone-company setting. Can I swing by with a tablet and show you a 5-minute demo? No forms, no contracts."

**In-person close:** hand them the tablet, show the demo staff screen, show the owner report. Leave the **one-pager** (the math box: $28.7K/yr average missed orders per US restaurant — TouchBistro/QSR; ~$38 avg takeout ticket).

## Objection handling
| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "We have online ordering." | "Great — this is for the people who still call. They call BECAUSE they want to talk to a human; when they hit a busy signal they don't switch to your site, they call the next pizza place. This catches them." |
| "Why share 15%?" | "You keep 85% of sales that today equal zero dollars. My cut pays for the numbers, the texting registration, the hosting, and my time — in months where recovered sales don't cover my costs, I eat the difference." |
| "What if it breaks?" | "If the service ever stopped working, a busy caller gets a busy signal — exactly what happens today. There is no new failure mode. And we test on YOUR lines before going live." |
| "Sounds like a scam / surveillance." | "It can't reach your website, register, or customer records. It only receives calls your lines already turned away. You own the texting account; I'm just the answering service." |
| "Corporate decides that." | "Fair — who handles guest experience for this region? One store pilot, your numbers decide the rollout." (chains: Nirchi's, Lupo's, Spiedie & Rib Pit, Dinosaur) |
| "Call me after the holidays." | "The holidays ARE the busy-line season — that's when you'll miss the most. Let me do the 2-week pilot NOW so your January report shows recovered dollars." |

## Monthly billing mechanic (keeps it professional + CAN-SPAM-safe)
- The counter tablet logs every recovered order. On the 1st, you send a **one-page report**: orders recovered, est. revenue, your 15%, plus a **Stripe payment link**.
- No chasing: the report IS the invoice. If they don't pay, you pause the service (their loss returns to the status quo — no hostage drama, just facts).

## Email sends (when Gmail is wired)
- Max **5 per day**, plain text, each unique (never template-blast), send Tue–Sat **9:00–10:00am ET**, owner-name greeting, reference THEIR menu/site in line 1, CAN-SPAM footer: real mailing address + "reply STOP to opt out".
- Subject lines that don't smell like sales: "busy signal Friday at 6:12" / "the orders [Name]'s phone lines turn away" / "question about your takeout line".

## Compliance rules (non-negotiable)
- Cold calls/emails to businesses only, respectful hours (2–4pm calls, morning emails), honor opt-outs immediately, truthful subjects/headers (CAN-SPAM), include physical mailing address on every email.
- Never overstate: cited stats come from vendor-funded studies (TouchBistro/QSR, Revmo, Hiya) — say "industry studies report," never "guaranteed."
