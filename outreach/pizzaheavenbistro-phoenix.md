Hi Pizza Heaven Bistro team — a midtown institution at 5150 N 7th St, family-owned since 1982, "Cleanest Kitchen in the Valley," and a homepage that says "Call to Inquire" for pickup and delivery deals. That's a 40-year phone-first operation.

Here's the leak: when rush hits and that line is tied up, the second caller gets a busy signal — and quietly orders elsewhere. You never see that loss.

The fix: when your line goes busy, an instant text fires back ("Sorry we missed you — text your order?") onto a counter tablet. Staff confirm it like any ticket, we log the recovered sale, and bill 15% of it. $0 upfront, $0 if nothing is recovered. No new number, no app, no training.

I'm a one-man operation out of Binghamton, NY, and I'm expanding the service beyond my home turf this quarter — Phoenix shops are first on the list.
