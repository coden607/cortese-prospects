Subject: Coloradough's 3–9 rush — the calls you don't see

Hi Coloradough team,

A takeout-and-delivery-only counter on E Smoky Hill Rd with a tight 3–9pm window — reviews describe call-ahead "parking lot" pickup. When dinner compresses into those hours, phones jam fast.

The quiet loss: a second caller who hits a busy signal doesn't wait — they order elsewhere, and you never see it.

I'm a one-man operation out of Binghamton, NY, expanding beyond my home turf this quarter — Aurora shops are first on the list.

The fix: a busy line instantly texts the caller — "Sorry we missed you — text your order?" — onto a counter tablet. Staff confirm it like any ticket; we log the recovered sale and bill 15% of it. $0 upfront, $0 if nothing's recovered. No new number, no app, no training.
