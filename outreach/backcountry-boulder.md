Subject: Backcountry's "call in first" line — never busy again

Hi Backcountry team,

Sixty-eight taps and Boulder's best wings a block from CU — your own homepage tells customers to call in first. On game-day rushes, that phone has to be melting.

Here's the leak: when a second caller hits a busy signal, they don't wait on hold — they quietly order elsewhere, and you never see that loss.

I'm a one-man operation out of Binghamton, NY, expanding beyond my home turf this quarter — Boulder shops are first on the list.

The fix: a busy line instantly texts the caller — "Sorry we missed you — text your order?" — onto a counter tablet. Staff confirm it like any ticket; we log the recovered sale and bill 15% of it. $0 upfront, $0 if nothing's recovered. No new number, no app, no training.
