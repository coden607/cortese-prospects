Hi Bavaro's team — a bustling Central Ave Neapolitan spot with the daily happy-hour crowd and a serious catering operation. You've got online ordering, which is great — but dinner-rush and catering calls still jam the phones.

Here's the part nobody sees: when rush hits and the line is tied up, the second caller gets a busy signal and quietly orders elsewhere. That sale is gone before you knew it existed.

The fix: when your line goes busy, an instant text fires back ("Sorry we missed you — text your order?") onto a counter tablet. Staff confirm it like any ticket, we log the recovered sale, and bill 15% of it. $0 upfront, $0 if nothing is recovered. No new number, no app, no training — it rides right alongside your existing setup.
